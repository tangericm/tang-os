> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans. Track completion below.

**Goal:** Replace Runner with a playable chess app and six engine difficulty levels.
**Architecture:** chess.js owns legal positions; a cancellable Stockfish worker owns search. GameWindow owns the board and current game, retaining the existing window lifecycle and `/play` URL.
**Tech Stack:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, chess.js 1.4.0, Stockfish.js 19.0.1 lite single-threaded, existing Playwright suite.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-11-03-chess-design.md

## Review Focus

- Reset, undo, changing difficulty, and minimizing during engine search must reject stale replies.
- Worker initialization/search failure must allow retry without losing the position.
- Playing Black and undoing the first engine move must not immediately replay it.
- Keyboard, mobile, and short-window layouts must keep board and controls usable.
- Promotion and terminal positions must block inappropriate moves and engine searches.
