## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, technical peers, and visitors exploring Eric M. Tang's work. The October 10, 2026 design choice confirmed this audience and a readable editorial portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Present Eric's computational imaging research and independent projects with real evidence, direct source/demo/paper links, and a clear path to his résumé and contact details. Add recent public work while keeping private material private.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js and React, static project routes, shared TypeScript project/profile/publication data, and Vercel deployment. Preserve published URLs, résumé PDF, contact details, scientific explainers, and the terminal and runner. The latest user direction preserves the desktop OS format while improving visuals, navigation, and structure. Keep project details in the warm dark desktop palette. Lead with peer-reviewed research, separate self-directed engineering from hobbies, and use short plain-language descriptions with optional technical detail. Build directly in code.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing public OCT images and scientific explainers; published research citations; Optical Design's reproducible Cooke triplet figure; Frankie Town's public playable game and code-only source, with its artwork served from the existing game; Eric's Recipe Book's live public website. No user-adoption, hiring-outcome, or new performance claims are established by this portfolio work.

## Product Principles

1. Let the actual work and its evidence lead.
2. Distinguish peer-reviewed research, self-directed engineering, and hobbies.
3. Keep facts in shared data and render useful HTML without JavaScript.
4. Publish only public project material; preserve source credit and experimental limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Explicit keyboard-accessible image comparisons, understandable workflow diagrams, and responsive reading on phones and desktops; semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and accessible navigation.
